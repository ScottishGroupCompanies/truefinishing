From being the go-to for floor prep, to high-value porcelain tile setting, to high-guarantee concrete leveling, you can count on True.

True Finishing is the Leading Concrete Flooring Contractor for Colorado. Based in Denver Colorado, we service the entire front range and help install outstanding polished concrete floors, epoxy flooring, concrete overlays and micro-toppings and more!

Established in 2007, True Finishing began as a company dedicated to concrete leveling and moisture mitigation. Over time, we steadily expanded our capabilities, evolving into the full-service flooring contractor we are today. Undoubtedly, as our team grows, so does our investment in specialized tooling and pumping equipment, allowing us to take on larger, more complex projects. As a result, we’ve completed thousands of successful installations and have earned a reputation as Denver’s trusted leader in polished concrete floors, epoxy flooring systems, moisture mitigation, and floor leveling.

## The Best Concrete Polishing Denver Has to Offer

As a result, we continue to be the leaders in concrete leveling and moisture mitigation systems in Denver. Over the years we have installed over 2,000,000 sq ft of Ardex moisture mitigation systems, we have never had a failure with any moisture mitigation systems installed. Likewise, our diligence with our preparation of concrete lends to true trust our clients get when working with us. Additionally, we always use superior products to achieve the best results possible.

Now a full-service decorative concrete flooring installer, at True Finishing we specialize in concrete polishing, concrete prep services, and epoxy flooring systems. Every one of our customers is extremely important to us and you are the reason for our success. Therefore, we guarantee all of our work and hope to impress you with our service.

Continually striving to improve our selves with continuing education, experimentation and just hard work, at True Finishing we take great pride in providing you long-lasting, beautiful results. Give us a call today for a free estimate and let us do a mock up to match your imagination!

**—Charles True**

## At True Finishing, We Are Floor Finishing Experts

### The Best Concrete Polishing & Floor Finishing in Denver

Looking for polished cement or finished concrete floors that elevate your space? True Finishing is Colorado’s trusted leader in concrete polishing and concrete finishing for commercial, industrial, and specialty spaces. Whether you’re designing a high-end showroom, a modern office, or a durable warehouse, our professional crews deliver flawless results with a commitment to excellence. In addition, we specialize in leveling concrete, moisture mitigation, and decorative finishes that bring both beauty and performance to your project.

### Durable Epoxy Flooring Systems – Denver’s Best

As the top provider of epoxy flooring in Denver, CO, True Finishing offers custom solutions built to last. From gym rubber flooring installations to high-performance coatings for warehouses, we tailor each job to meet your vision and usage needs. Also, we work closely with general contractors and commercial clients to deliver seamless, long-lasting results. Every epoxy floor is installed using premium materials and top-tier prep techniques for durability you can count on.

Beyond concrete and epoxy, True Finishing is proud to provide carpet installation in Denver for commercial spaces, bringing softness, acoustics, and style to your interior design. Our commercial finishing services cover a full range of surfaces, ensuring your floors are both beautiful and built to perform under pressure. Moreover, every project we complete is backed by a solid guarantee and a proven track record of success. For this reason, we’re the company to call when you want the job done right.
