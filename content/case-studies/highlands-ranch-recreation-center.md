### Request for Service

This was a highly challenging project due to a tight 7-day timeframe, though it was a challenge True Finishing had no problems rising to the occasion for. The existing floor had accumulated over 200 layers of wax, which required complete removal before polishing. With stained joints and other complex elements, completing everything within one week seemed impossible. However, through careful planning and strategic sectioning, we tackled the task head-on. Our team also installed a new Micro-Topping on two large staircases and replaced the entire rubber wall base in the area.

* * *

### Solutions that Delivered Stunning Results

Faced with the time constraints, our team of experts ran double shifts, strategically completing sections before the facility’s shutdown. By doing so, we ensured the space was fully transformed within the deadline. The results were stunning: polished and stained concrete floors, refreshed staircases with micro-topping and new stair treads, all enhanced by sleek new rubber wall base.
