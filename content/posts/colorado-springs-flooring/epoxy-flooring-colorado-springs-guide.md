## What is Epoxy Flooring?

Epoxy flooring is a surface composed of multiple layers of epoxy resin applied to a floor base. This combination creates a resilient, long-lasting surface that stands up to chemicals, stains, and wear. Unlike traditional flooring, epoxy bonds exceptionally well with concrete, ensuring that the coating doesn’t peel or crack under pressure. The result is a seamless, glossy finish that is both beautiful and functional.
