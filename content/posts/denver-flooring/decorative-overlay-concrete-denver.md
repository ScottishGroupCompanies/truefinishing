## What is a Decorative Concrete Overlay?

A decorative concrete overlay is a thin layer of specially formulated polymer-modified cement applied over existing concrete surfaces. Whether you’re dealing with indoor floors or outdoor patios, these overlays allow you to mask imperfections and add texture, color, and design to dull, faded concrete. Best of all, they’re more than just a pretty face—they also help reinforce the underlying substrate.

In fact, according to a study published in the [Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/journal/jmcee), decorative overlays can improve the durability of aging concrete surfaces by an average of 35%, significantly extending the life of the structure beneath.

## Types of Decorative Concrete Overlays

At True Finishes, we offer a wide array of overlay options to meet your design goals and performance needs. Here are some of the most popular types we install:

### Stamped Concrete Overlay

Stamped overlays replicate the appearance of high-end materials like brick, tile, stone, or wood using special stamps and coloring techniques. These are ideal for outdoor patios, driveways, and pool decks.

### Microtoppings

Microtoppings are thin overlays used to cover small surface imperfections, offering a smooth or lightly textured finish. These are great for interior floors where a sleek modern appearance is desired.

### Spray-Down Systems

Spray-down overlays are typically used on pool decks and outdoor walkways, offering excellent slip resistance and creative designs through tape or stencil patterns.

### Self-Leveling Overlays

These overlays are perfect for severely damaged or uneven concrete surfaces. They flow evenly to create a smooth base layer, often used in garages, basements, and commercial interiors.
