## What Is Self-Leveling Concrete?

Self-leveling concrete is a polymer-modified cement that flows easily and sets quickly, forming a flat, smooth surface that requires very little aftercare. It’s used to repair uneven floors, prepare surfaces for overlays or epoxy coatings, and restore compromised concrete without the need for expensive full replacement. Unlike traditional concrete that requires manual leveling, self-leveling compounds spread uniformly under their own weight, requiring minimal labor and reducing human error.
