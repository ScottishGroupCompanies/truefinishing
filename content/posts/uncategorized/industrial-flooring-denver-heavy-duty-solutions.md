## Types of Heavy-Duty Industrial Flooring We Offer

At True Finishes, we offer several industrial flooring systems engineered for high performance. Each solution is tailored to meet your facility’s functional, safety, and aesthetic goals.

### 1\. Epoxy Flooring Systems

Epoxy coatings are among the most popular choices for industrial settings, and for good reason. These systems offer excellent resistance to chemicals, abrasions, and heavy foot or forklift traffic. According to a [2019 study published in the Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/journal/jmcee4), heavy-duty epoxy flooring systems can reduce lifecycle maintenance costs by up to 30% compared to traditional concrete floors. This makes epoxy flooring not only a smart investment but also a sustainable one.

### 2\. Urethane Cement Flooring

Ideal for environments like commercial kitchens or food manufacturing facilities, urethane cement flooring stands up to extreme temperature changes and aggressive cleaning processes. It offers both slip-resistant textures and antimicrobial properties, perfect for sanitation-sensitive industries.

### 3\. Polished Concrete

Polished concrete is an excellent low-maintenance option for facilities that need durable, highly reflective flooring. It’s long-lasting, cost-effective, and provides a clean, modern appearance, making it popular in commercial and distribution environments.

## Benefits of Heavy-Duty Industrial Flooring

Choosing the right industrial flooring in Denver goes beyond material selection—it’s about performance and long-term value. Here are several reasons why upgrading to heavy-duty flooring with True Finishes is smart for your business:

-   **Durability:** Our industrial flooring solutions are designed to handle extreme loads, high traffic, spills, and impact—ensuring longevity and reducing downtime for repairs.
-   **Safety:** According to the [National Safety Council](https://www.nsc.org/work-safety), facilities that implement advanced heavy-duty flooring have reported up to a 20% reduction in slip and fall incidents. Non-slip textures and chemical-resistant coatings further enhance safety and regulatory compliance.
-   **Cost Savings:** Reducing maintenance needs can translate into major savings over time. As noted in the Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering, using epoxy flooring could cut maintenance costs by up to 30% over the life of the floor.
-   **Hygiene:** Our seamless flooring systems are easier to clean, resist microbial growth, and support sanitary standards in food production and healthcare environments.
