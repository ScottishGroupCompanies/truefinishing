## What Is a Decorative Concrete Overlay?

A decorative concrete overlay is a thin layer of specially formulated concrete that is applied over existing concrete surfaces. This layer, usually ranging from 1/8″ to 3/8″ thick, is textured, stamped, or stained to mimic the appearance of natural stone, brick, tile, or even wood. It is a resourceful way to enhance outdoor patios, driveways, walkways, and even interior spaces with custom design and durable performance.

## Why Denver Homeowners and Businesses Choose Overlays

Modern concrete overlays are not only beautiful—they are engineered for long-term durability and resistance against Colorado’s freeze-thaw cycles. For urban environments like [Denver](/cities-we-serve/), concrete overlays are a cost-effective solution to maintain architectural appeal while minimizing long-term maintenance.

In fact, experts from the [National Ready Mixed Concrete Association](https://www.nrmca.org) report that decorative overlays can reduce maintenance costs by as much as 40% when compared to full concrete replacement. That makes overlays an ideal option for high-traffic sidewalks, parking lots, and municipal spaces throughout Denver.
