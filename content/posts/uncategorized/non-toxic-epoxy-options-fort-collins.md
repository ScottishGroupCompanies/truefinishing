## Why Opt for Non-Toxic Epoxy?

The use of traditional epoxy coatings can often lead to the emission of \*\*volatile organic compounds (VOCs)\*\*, which are harmful chemicals contributing to indoor air pollution. According to a study published in Environmental Science & Technology, [low-VOC, non-toxic epoxy coatings can reduce VOC emissions by as much as 80%](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.est.7b01090) compared to conventional solvent-based epoxies.

Moreover, the US Environmental Protection Agency highlights that utilizing low-VOC, non-toxic epoxy coatings can lead to a reduction in indoor VOC concentrations by around 50%, thereby significantly improving [indoor air quality](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/volatile-organic-compounds-impact-indoor-air-quality).

## Healthier Choices for Fort Collins Residents

In Fort Collins, a city renowned for its dedication to maintaining sustainability and health, switching to non-toxic epoxies is a choice that aligns with these values. Research reported in the Journal of Cleaner Production indicates that [switching to non-toxic epoxy formulations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2019.02.123) in industrial applications can cut workers’ exposure to hazardous chemicals by approximately 75%, thus supporting improved occupational health.
