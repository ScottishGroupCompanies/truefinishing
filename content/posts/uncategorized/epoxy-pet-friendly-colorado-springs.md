## What Makes Epoxy Flooring Pet Friendly?

Epoxy flooring is created by combining resin and hardener that chemically react to form a durable plastic coating. Applied over concrete, it cures to create a sleek and resilient surface. But how exactly does epoxy flooring support a pet-friendly lifestyle?

-   **Scratch and Stain Resistance:** Epoxy flooring holds up against nail scratches that might destroy hardwood and resists stains from pet accidents.
-   **Easy Cleanup:** Spilled water bowls or unexpected messes clean up with just a mop or paper towel—no special cleaners required.
-   **Allergy Relief:** Unlike carpet, it doesn’t trap pet dander, mites, or fur, promoting better indoor air quality for allergic family members.
-   **Temperature-tolerant:** Epoxy floors stay cooler in warm months and respond well to radiant heating in colder seasons, keeping paws comfortable year-round.
