## Why Garage Epoxy Prep Matters

The effectiveness of any epoxy floor coating heavily depends on the condition of the concrete beneath it. According to the [American Concrete Institute](https://www.concrete.org/publications/internationalconcreteabstractsportal.aspx?m=details&i=51683319), proper floor preparation can improve epoxy adhesion by up to 30%. This not only enhances durability but also prevents common issues like peeling, bubbling, and cracking.

On the flip side, a study by the [International Concrete Repair Institute (ICRI)](https://www.icri.org/page/PublicationsGuidelines) shows that poor surface prep contributes to a 40% increase in epoxy coating failures, especially in commercial and high-traffic environments. That’s a costly mistake you can’t afford to make—whether you’re upgrading your home’s garage or revamping an industrial facility.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Proper Garage Epoxy Prep

At True Finishes, we follow a detailed approach to prep concrete surfaces for epoxy coatings. Below are the key steps we take as part of every professional garage epoxy project in Colorado Springs.

### 1\. Thorough Cleaning

We start by removing all oil, grease, dirt, and debris from the concrete. Surface contaminants can prevent the epoxy from bonding properly. This stage includes power washing, degreasing, and vacuuming to ensure the garage floor is spotless. Any existing coatings or sealants are also removed using grinders or chemical strippers.

### 2\. Concrete Repair

Before coating, it’s crucial to repair any existing cracks or pits in the concrete. Damaged surfaces create weak spots in the epoxy layer and compromise overall longevity. Our experienced technicians use high-strength repair materials to ensure a smooth, level finish across your entire garage floor.

### 3\. Mechanical Grinding

Achieving proper surface profile is essential for epoxy adhesion. We use industrial-grade diamond grinders to mechanically etch the surface. This process not only removes residual coatings but also opens up the concrete pores, enabling deep penetration of the primer coat.

In fact, a [study published in the Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/%28ASCE%29MT.1943-5533.0002347) found that floors treated with etching and grinding exhibited up to 50% more micro-roughness, significantly increasing the bond strength of epoxy coatings.

### 4\. Moisture Testing

Colorado’s climate can lead to hidden moisture in concrete slabs, especially during spring thaws or after heavy rain. Moisture vapor emissions may cause your epoxy to bubble or delaminate. At True Finishes, we conduct comprehensive moisture tests to ensure your concrete is dry and suitable for coating. If needed, we apply moisture vapor barriers to protect against underlying humidity issues.

### 5\. Final Inspection and Surface Profiling

Before we begin the epoxy application, we perform a final inspection to verify the proper [surface profile](/floor-preparation/) has been achieved. According to ICRI guidelines, the desired profile (Concrete Surface Profile or CSP) for epoxy ranges from CSP 2 to CSP 3, depending on the coating thickness. Our team uses specialized tools to gauge and ensure consistent prep across the garage floor.
