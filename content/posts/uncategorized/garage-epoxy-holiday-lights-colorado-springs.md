## Why Choose Epoxy for Your Garage Floor?

One of the standout benefits of garage epoxy from True Finishes is its ability to extend the service life of garage floors by up to 150%, as noted in a study from the [Journal of Protective Coatings & Linings](https://www.paint.org/). ## Maximize Your Storage Solutions

Once your garage floor is coated with epoxy, organizing and storing holiday decorations, like lights and ornaments, becomes significantly more efficient. The smooth, level surface means you can safely place storage boxes without worrying about the uneven ground beneath potentially damaging the contents.

Additionally, when it’s time to retrieve decorations next season, the sturdy epoxy floor will hold up against the weight of moving boxes, reducing the concern over scratches or unsightly stains. Another advantage is that epoxy floors are resistant to moisture, which is essential in protecting your stored holiday items from Colorado’s unpredictable weather conditions.
