## The Science and Advantages of Metallic Sapphire Epoxy

Metallic epoxy floors are renowned not just for their beauty, but also for their technical superiority. A [study published in the Journal of Coatings Technology](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11998-012-9455-6) highlights that advanced metallic epoxy coatings can extend the service life of treated surfaces by approximately 35% over conventional coatings. This is a game-changer for both residential and commercial spaces, offering long-lasting protection against wear and tear.

Moreover, research from the [American Chemical Society](https://pubs.acs.org) indicates that metallic epoxy coatings, such as our Brilliant Sapphire variant, improve abrasion resistance by up to 40%. This enhanced durability is essential for areas that experience heavy foot traffic, such as retail spaces, restaurants, and busy household kitchens.

## Efficiency in Application

Another vital aspect of using metallic epoxy is the efficiency in installation and curing. A recent report featured in [Construction and Building Materials](https://www.journals.elsevier.com/construction-and-building-materials) observed that projects utilizing metallic epoxy coatings experienced an average reduction in project timelines by about 20%. Faster installation and curing times mean less disruption to your daily activities and quicker access to your enhanced space.
