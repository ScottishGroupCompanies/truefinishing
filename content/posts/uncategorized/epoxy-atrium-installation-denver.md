## The Benefits of Epoxy Flooring for Atriums

Epoxy flooring offers numerous advantages, particularly in high-traffic areas like atriums. A significant study published in the [Journal of Coatings Technology and Research](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11998-012-9423-7) highlights that epoxy floor systems enhance durability by 60% over traditional coatings. This makes it an ideal choice for environments that experience massive daily footfall, ensuring the flooring remains pristine for years.

Moreover, a survey by the [National Association of Home Builders](https://www.nahb.org) revealed that over 65% of commercial atrium projects in urban [Denver](/cities-we-serve/floor-finishing-concrete-polishing-denver-co/) prefer epoxy flooring. This is due to its superior safety features, such as slip-resistance, and the reduced maintenance needs.
