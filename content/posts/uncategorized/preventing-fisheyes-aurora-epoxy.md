When it comes to achieving a flawless concrete surface with epoxy coatings, one of the most frustrating challenges faced by professionals and DIY enthusiasts alike is the occurrence of fisheyes. These unsightly craters can appear on the surface of coatings, causing imperfections that mar the aesthetic appeal and compromise the functional quality of the finish. As experts in concrete coatings in Aurora, True Finishes is dedicated to helping you prevent fisheyes and achieve that perfect finish you desire.

Fisheyes occur when contaminants on the surface or within the coating itself create areas that repel the coating, leading to these unattractive indentations. Fortunately, by understanding the causes and implementing proper techniques, you can significantly reduce the risk of fisheye formation in your epoxy coatings. Let’s delve into effective strategies and tips for preventing fisheyes during your next concrete coating project in Aurora.

## Understanding the Causes of Fisheyes

The formation of fisheyes in epoxy coatings often stems from surface contamination, moisture, and improper mixing or application techniques. According to a [study published in the Journal of Coatings Technology and Research](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/15475707), one of the primary culprits behind fisheye formation is inadequate surface preparation. Thorough cleaning prior to epoxy application can reduce fisheye defects by up to 70%. Proper cleaning ensures that contaminants such as oils, dust, and debris are removed, which in turn enhances the adhesion of the epoxy.

## Surface Preparation and Cleaning

Before starting your epoxy coating project, it’s crucial to invest time in preparing the concrete surface. Begin by using a degreaser to remove any oil or grease spots. Follow this up with a pressure wash to clear away dust and debris, ensuring the surface is completely clean. According to research by [NACE International](https://www.nace.org/resources/corrosion-central), effectively managing substrate moisture and eliminating surface contaminants can decrease the risk of fisheye formation by approximately 60%.

For those working on concrete surfaces in [Aurora](/cities-we-serve/), where humidity levels can fluctuate, it’s vital to ensure the substrate is dry before applying any coating. Utilizing a moisture meter to verify the dryness of the surface can be a beneficial step in preventing fisheyes.

## Optimal Mixing and Application Techniques

The method of mixing and applying epoxy also plays a significant role in the prevention of fisheyes. A [technical paper by the American Concrete Institute](https://www.concrete.org/) highlights that using optimal mixing methods and maintaining proper curing conditions can lead to nearly an 80% reduction in fisheye occurrences. This involves ensuring that the components of the epoxy are thoroughly mixed according to the manufacturer’s instructions, without introducing air bubbles.

During application, apply the epoxy in thin, even coats using a suitable roller or brush. Working in smaller sections can help maintain control over the application, ensuring a more uniform layer that is less prone to defects.

## Avoiding Contaminated Tools and Environment

Even if you meticulously clean the surface, using contaminated tools can reintroduce agents that cause fisheyes. Ensure all tools and equipment are clean and free from previous coatings or contaminants. The environment where you’re conducting the application also matters; a dust-free, controlled setting can lead to better results.
