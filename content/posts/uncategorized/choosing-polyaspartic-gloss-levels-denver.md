## Understanding Gloss Levels

Gloss levels in flooring range from matte to high-gloss, each offering distinct visual and functional characteristics:

-   **Matte:** Ideal for reducing glare, matte finishes are recommended for areas with high exposure to natural light. These finishes emphasize a room’s natural beauty without reflecting too much light.
-   **Mid-Gloss:** Combining aesthetic appeal with increased durability, mid-gloss finishes are popular in Denver. A study by [Floor Covering News](https://www.floorcoveringnews.com/) showed that 78% of property owners in climates similar to Denver’s favor mid-gloss finishes.
-   **High-Gloss:** Providing a polished and modern look, high-gloss finishes are often used for decorative purposes or in settings where a pristine appearance is desired.

## The Importance of Gloss Level in Denver

Denver’s unique climate and environmental conditions influence the choice of gloss level. With its high elevation and abundant sunlight, glare becomes a significant consideration. The [National Safety Council](https://www.nsc.org/) notes that lower-gloss finishes can reduce glare-related accidents by up to 24% in brightly lit industrial environments, reinforcing the need for careful selection of gloss levels in Denver.

## Where to Use Different Gloss Levels

At True Finishes, we provide polyaspartic flooring solutions tailored to your specific needs. Here’s how different gloss levels vary in application:

-   **Residential Homes:** Mid-gloss finishes offer a perfect blend of durability and beauty, making them ideal for living rooms and kitchens. These finishes are favored by many Denver homeowners, aligning with the local preference noted by Floor Covering News.
-   **Commercial Spaces:** High-gloss finishes can create an upscale look, suitable for showrooms or retail spaces where aesthetics are paramount. However, consider the impact of Denver’s sunny climate, as it may accentuate glare.
-   **Industrial Settings:** Matte or low-gloss finishes enhance safety by minimizing glare and improving traction, important in fast-paced industrial environments.
