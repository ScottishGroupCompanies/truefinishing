## The Allure of Metallic Onyx Epoxy

There’s something utterly mesmerizing about the play of colors in onyx metallic epoxy floors. The intricate swirls and depth of color mimic the appearance of natural stone, creating a luxurious aesthetic that can elevate any environment. Whether you’re aiming to add a modern touch to a residential living area or seeking to impress clients in a commercial space, metallic onyx epoxy can meet your needs.

Metallic epoxy floors are created using a special multi-layering technique that includes a combination of metallic powders or pigments within the epoxy resin. The result is a floor with unique depth and reflective quality, producing a dynamic, ever-changing visual depending on the angle of light and viewing.
