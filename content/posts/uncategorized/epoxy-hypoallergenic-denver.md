## Understanding Hypoallergenic Epoxy

Traditional epoxy resins often contain volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and other chemicals that can trigger allergic reactions in sensitive individuals. Fortunately, hypoallergenic epoxy formulations have been designed to minimize these risks. A 2020 study published in the [International Journal of Occupational and Environmental Health](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32032981/) found that using hypoallergenic epoxy reduced the incidence of contact allergic reactions by up to 60% among workers in high-exposure environments.

## Benefits of Hypoallergenic Epoxy in Denver

Besides being allergy-friendly, hypoallergenic epoxies offer several benefits that make them an ideal choice for various applications in Denver and beyond. From reduced VOC emissions to enhanced durability, here’s what makes them stand out:

-   **Reduced Allergens:** Hypoallergenic epoxy formulations can significantly lower contact dermatitis rates compared to conventional epoxy resins. According to a review in the [Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology](https://www.jacionline.org/article/S0091-6749(20)31185-8/fulltext), adopting these alternatives has shown a marked decrease in allergy symptoms among exposed workers.
-   **Environmental Safety:** The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency reports a 40% reduction in VOC emissions in indoor environments treated with hypoallergenic epoxy coatings. This reduction contributes to better indoor air quality, potentially decreasing triggers of allergy and asthma.
-   **Durability and Aesthetics:** Our hypoallergenic epoxy options don’t compromise on quality. They offer the same strength and visual appeal you expect from a premium epoxy flooring solution, making your Denver space stylish and resilient.
