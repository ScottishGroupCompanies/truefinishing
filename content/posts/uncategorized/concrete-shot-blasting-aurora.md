## What is Concrete Shot Blasting?

Shot blasting is a process that involves propelling a stream of abrasive media at high velocity to clean and prepare the surface of concrete. This method not only removes surface contaminants but also creates a rough texture on the surface, which is essential for ensuring effective bonding of repair overlays or coatings.

## Why Choose Concrete Shot Blasting in Aurora?

Research published in the [Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/abs/10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9399(2007)133:6(816)) has demonstrated that shot blasting can enhance the surface profile of concrete by up to 25%. This improvement significantly boosts the bond strength of repair overlays when compared to traditional mechanical methods. Furthermore, the [National Ready Mixed Concrete Association](https://www.nrmca.org/tech-briefs) highlights that utilizing shot blasting can reduce processing time by approximately 40%, contributing to enhanced project efficiency and reduced labor costs.

Moreover, [Concrete Construction Magazine](https://www.concreteconstruction.net) reports that shot blasting can eliminate up to 95% of surface contaminants. This makes it an ideal choice for preparing concrete surfaces to receive coatings or repair systems effectively.
