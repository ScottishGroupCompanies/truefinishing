Concrete spalling, a common issue in Aurora, requires proactive measures to ensure durability, especially before applying an epoxy coating. At True Finishes, we understand the intricacies involved in preparing surfaces for epoxy application, making us the go-to experts for [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/). Whether you’re aiming to enhance your office, warehouse, or home, addressing spalling first is critical. Let’s delve into why concrete spalling repair is crucial before epoxy application.

Spalling, or the chipping and flaking of concrete, occurs due to exposure to moisture, freeze-thaw cycles, or poor concrete consistency. In Aurora’s unique climate, these factors sometimes converge, urging residents to stay vigilant. Proactive repair of spalling before considering epoxy paintings not only increases aesthetic appeal but also ensures the longevity and safety of the flooring.

## The Importance of Surface Preparation

A well-prepared surface is the backbone of any successful epoxy application. Research published in [Construction and Building Materials](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095006181930404X) (2019) found that proper surface preparation can enhance bond strength by up to 35%. Addressing spalling is a critical first step in this preparation process, paving the way for epoxy application.

At True Finishes, we adhere to industry standards that align with findings from the [American Concrete Institute](https://www.concrete.org/store/productdetail.aspx?ItemID=3430). They report that inadequate surface preparation contributes to more than 40% of premature concrete spalling. Our meticulous approach ensures that your flooring is shielded from such pitfalls.

## Steps in Concrete Spalling Repair

Before proceeding with epoxy, here are the steps we undertake to repair spalling:

### 1\. Detailed Inspection

Our repair process begins with a thorough inspection of your concrete surface to assess the extent of the spalling damage. We understand that each project has its unique requirements, taking into account Aurora’s specific environmental challenges.

### 2\. Surface Cleaning

An essential step involves meticulous cleaning to eradicate dirt, grime, and other contaminants. This step aligns with the methodology noted in a survey of structural repair professionals, which indicated that cleanliness and profiling can boost repair longevity by 45% ([American Society of Civil Engineers](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/civil-engineering/)).

### 3\. Removal of Loose Concrete

Loose, flaky parts of the spalled concrete are carefully removed, ensuring that the sturdy foundation left behind can support the new epoxy application.

### 4\. Concrete Patching

We use high-quality repair mixtures to patch and fill the voids created by spalling, smoothing the surface for subsequent treatments.

### 5\. Profiling the Surface

Surface profiling through abrasion or the use of acid etching provides a rough texture crucial for epoxy adhesion. This step further aligns with expert findings about the critical nature of surface readiness.
