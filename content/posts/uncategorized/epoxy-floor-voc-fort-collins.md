## What Are VOCs and Why Do They Matter?

Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) are chemicals that easily evaporate at room temperature. They are commonly used in paints, lacquers, and epoxy coatings. When applied in residential and commercial spaces, these compounds can off-gas into the air, contributing to indoor air pollution. Short-term exposure to VOCs can cause headaches, dizziness, and nausea, while long-term exposure has been linked to more serious health concerns, including respiratory diseases and even cancer.

According to the [U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/volatile-organic-compounds-impact-indoor-air-quality), low-VOC epoxy coatings can reduce VOC emissions by up to 90% compared to traditional solvent-based formulations. This not only boosts overall indoor air quality but also safeguards the health of your household, making it a smart choice for Fort Collins homeowners.
