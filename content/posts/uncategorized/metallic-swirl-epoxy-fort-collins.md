## What Is Metallic Swirl Epoxy Flooring?

Metallic swirl epoxy is a type of decorative epoxy coating that uses a blend of metallic pigments—often mixed into a two-component epoxy resin—to create flowing, three-dimensional effects that resemble molten metal, marble, or even clouds. The application process involves manipulating the coating during curing to achieve patterns that are truly one-of-a-kind. No two floors ever look exactly alike!

This artistic flooring solution is not only beautiful but also highly functional. Thanks to its seamless surface, resistant properties, and ability to stand up against daily wear and tear, it’s perfect for high-traffic commercial settings and modern residential applications alike.

## How to Create Stunning Metallic Swirl Epoxy Patterns

Creating metallic swirl epoxy requires both technical precision and an artistic eye. At True Finishes, our experts follow a detailed process to ensure flawless results for every project:

### 1\. Surface Preparation

Preparation is crucial for long-lasting metallic epoxy flooring. We begin by grinding or shot-blasting the concrete surface to remove any imperfections and create a rough surface for better adhesion.

### 2\. Base Coat Application

Next, a solid-color base coat is applied, typically in a dark or contrasting shade to make the metallic pigments pop. This coat provides the foundation for the swirl effects.

### 3\. Metallic Pigment Pouring

We then mix metallic pigments with the clear epoxy resin and pour it over the base coat. Using various tools—rollers, squeegees, brushes—we manipulate the epoxy to create mesmerizing swirls, veins, ripples, and depth-enhancing effects.

### 4\. Sealing and Curing

Once the desired design is achieved, we allow the epoxy to self-level and cure. A protective topcoat is added for extra durability, enhancing the floor’s resistance to scratches, stains, and chemicals.

The result? A floor that’s not only a functional surface but also a centerpiece of your space.
