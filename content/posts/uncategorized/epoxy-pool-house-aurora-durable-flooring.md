## Why Choose Epoxy Flooring?

Epoxy flooring has been lauded for its exceptional durability and aesthetic appeal. According to [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/epoxy-flooring-market), the global epoxy flooring market was valued at approximately USD 1.6 billion in 2019 and is forecasted to grow at a CAGR of around 6.1% over the forecast period. This growth is a testament to the widespread recognition of epoxy flooring’s benefits.

Featuring a unique combination of resin and hardener, epoxy floors create a seamless and robust surface. In a [study published in the Journal of Coatings Technology and Research](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/09546634.2013.858315), it was revealed that epoxy flooring systems could provide up to a 30% improvement in abrasion resistance compared to traditional concrete floors. This durability makes them particularly suited for the hustle and bustle of a pool area.

## The Benefits of Epoxy Flooring for Pool Houses

For pool house owners in urban settings like Aurora, choosing the right flooring is crucial. A survey by a leading North American flooring association found that 78% of pool house owners in urban areas reported higher satisfaction with epoxy flooring in terms of performance and aesthetics when compared to conventional options ([source](https://www.epoxyflooring.org)). So what makes epoxy such a favored choice?

### 1\. Water and Chemical Resistance

Pool environments are inherently wet and need a flooring solution that can withstand water exposure. Epoxy floors are non-porous, meaning they resist water penetration, reducing the risk of mold and mildew growth. Additionally, they’re resistant to many chemicals found in pool maintenance, ensuring your floor remains pristine.

### 2\. Enhanced Durability

Pool houses see a lot of traffic, from families spending their weekends by the pool to guests at social gatherings. Epoxy flooring’s superior durability ensures that your floors can withstand heavy footfall, making them a long-term investment. For more information on our epoxy floor offerings, visit our [epoxy floors page](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/).

### 3\. Customizable Aesthetics

Epoxy flooring isn’t just functional; it’s also beautiful. Available in a myriad of colors, patterns, and finishes, it can be customized to match the aesthetic of your pool house. Whether you desire the sleek look of a glossy finish or the understated elegance of a matte option, epoxy can provide the style you seek.

### 4\. Low Maintenance

Once installed, epoxy floors require minimal maintenance. Unlike other flooring options that may require regular sealing or waxing, epoxy floors stay beautiful with basic cleaning, saving you time and effort.
