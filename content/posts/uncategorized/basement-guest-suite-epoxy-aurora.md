## Epoxy Flooring Installation Process

The installation of epoxy flooring is a specialized process that ensures durability and an attractive finish. Here’s what you can expect when you choose True Finishes for your basement guest suite in Aurora:

### 1\. Surface Preparation

Before epoxy application, the concrete surface must be thoroughly cleaned and prepared. This involves removing existing coatings, repairing cracks, and ensuring the surface is moisture-free.

### 2\. Epoxy Application

After preparation, our team expertly applies the epoxy coating. The process involves multiple layers to achieve the desired thickness, finish, and color, tailored to your specific preferences and the requirements of your guest suite.

### 3\. Curing and Sealing

Once the epoxy is applied, it needs to cure. A sealing layer is also typically applied to enhance durability and appearance. This step ensures the finished floor is resilient against wear and tear.
