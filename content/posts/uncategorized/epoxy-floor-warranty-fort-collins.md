## What Is an Epoxy Floor Warranty?

An epoxy floor warranty is a written guarantee issued by your contractor or the product manufacturer, covering potential defects in materials or workmanship for a specified period. The specifics of your epoxy floor warranty will depend on the provider, the application method, and environmental conditions. Typical warranties range from one to fifteen years, and may cover issues like peeling, bubbling, discoloration, or delamination.

## What’s Typically Covered in an Epoxy Floor Warranty?

Warranties can vary slightly based on the provider, but most reputable epoxy floor warranties in Fort Collins cover the following:

-   **Peeling or Delamination**: The top-coated surface separates from the substrate.
-   **Bubbling or Blistering**: Caused by poor installation or trapped moisture.
-   **Discoloration**: Abnormal fading or yellowing under standard conditions.
-   **Cracking**: Beyond normal structural movement or settling.

Some warranties may also include labor costs for repairs, while others may only cover materials. The specifics are important, so be sure to review your contract in detail.

## What Can Void Your Warranty?

While an epoxy floor warranty offers great protection, it can be nullified if certain conditions are not met. Here are some common reasons epoxy warranties are voided:

-   **DIY Repairs or Modifications**: Performing unauthorized repairs can void the warranty.
-   **Improper Cleaning Products**: Harsh chemicals or acids can degrade the epoxy coating.
-   **Heavy Equipment Misuse**: Using equipment beyond the floor’s load tolerance.
-   **Neglected Maintenance**: Ignoring cleaning or resealing schedules.

To maintain your warranty’s validity, follow all care guidelines provided by your installer. At True Finishes, we ensure our clients are educated on proper maintenance immediately after installation.
