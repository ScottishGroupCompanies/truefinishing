## What Is Hot Tire Pickup?

Hot tire pickup occurs when the heat from your vehicle’s tires—often after driving—softens the garage floor coating. As tires cool, they contract slightly and can pull the coating away from the concrete, leading to unsightly peeling and degradation. This is especially common in areas like Aurora where changing temperatures challenge flooring durability year-round.

## Why Hot Tire Pickup Happens

The root causes of hot tire pickup typically include:

-   Inadequate surface preparation before epoxy application
-   Low-quality or incorrectly formulated coatings
-   Poor bonding between the coating and concrete substrate
-   Extreme surface temperatures from vehicles parked after use

Thankfully, with advances in epoxy science and professional installation techniques, these problems can be mitigated or eliminated altogether.
