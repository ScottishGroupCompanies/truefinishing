## Why Moisture in Concrete Is a Problem in Colorado Springs

Colorado Springs features a semi-arid climate with fluctuating weather patterns, which can create ideal conditions for concrete moisture infiltration. Groundwater seepage, sudden temperature shifts, and inconsistent humidity levels can all contribute to elevated moisture levels in concrete slabs. Left untreated, these issues can cause adhesive failures, mold and mildew growth, flooring deterioration, and even structural damage.

These risks are magnified during Denver’s wet seasons or when homes are built over high water tables or poorly prepared subfloors. Homeowners and contractors may wrongly assume that dry air naturally eliminates moisture problems, but moisture vapor emissions from concrete can persist for years after the initial pour.

## Warning Signs of Concrete Moisture Problems

It’s important to recognize early indicators of moisture issues in concrete:

-   Peeling floor finishes and bubbling adhesives
-   Discoloration or damp spots on floors
-   Moldy odors or visible mildew
-   Efflorescence (white powdery residue)
-   Unstable or warped flooring, especially with wood and vinyl materials

If you’re seeing these signs in your [Colorado Springs](/cities-we-serve/colorado-springs-concrete-floor-finishing/) home, it’s time to take action.

### 2\. Moisture Barriers and Sealants

According to industry guidelines, installing appropriate moisture barriers can reduce moisture penetration in concrete by up to [60%](https://www.concreteconstruction.net/products/concrete-producers/moisture-management-best-practices_o). At True Finishes, we apply professional-grade sealants and vapor barriers that significantly reduce moisture migration from the subfloor to your finished flooring.

### 3\. Drainage and Waterproofing Solutions

We also assess your property’s exterior and interior drainage systems. Proper grading, vapor retarders, and sealing techniques can reduce the chances of mold and mildew forming on or below your concrete slab by nearly [50%](https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-course), effectively protecting indoor air quality and building health.

### 4\. Advanced Moisture Testing

Moisture issues can’t be resolved based on guesswork. We use advanced testing technologies that include RH (Relative Humidity) probes, calcium chloride testing, and thermal scanning to determine the precise moisture content and pinpoint problem areas in your slab.
