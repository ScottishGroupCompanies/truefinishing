## Why Epoxy Flooring?

Epoxy coatings offer an unmatched combination of cost-effectiveness and strength. According to industry studies, [epoxy floors can extend the life of concrete surfaces up to 10 times longer](https://www.concretenetwork.com/how-to-most/epoxy-flooring-guide/) compared to uncoated concrete. This longevity, combined with low maintenance requirements and resistance to stains, makes epoxy an excellent choice for high-traffic areas like garages, basements, and industrial spaces.

## Step 1: On-Site Consultation and Evaluation

The first step in our epoxy floor installation process begins with a free consultation. During this visit, the True Finishes team evaluates your existing concrete surface, assesses moisture levels, checks for damage or irregularities, and confirms which type of epoxy system will work best for your needs. Whether it’s a flake system, metallic epoxy, or a high-build solid color option, we tailor each project to suit your aesthetic and functional requirements.

## Step 2: Surface Preparation – The Foundation of Quality

Proper surface preparation is arguably the most critical step in any epoxy floor installation. In fact, [35-40% of the total installation cost is typically attributed to this process](https://www.epoxyflooring.org/cost/). This involves cleaning, grinding, repairing cracks, and ensuring an optimal surface profile for epoxy adhesion. We use advanced diamond grinding machines and industrial vacuums to remove contaminants and open the pores of the concrete. A clean and coarse surface is key for the epoxy to properly bond and perform for years to come.

To learn more about our meticulous approach to preparation, visit our [Floor Preparation page](/floor-preparation/).

## Step 3: Primer Application

After surface prep, we apply a quality epoxy primer. This layer acts as the bonding bridge between the raw concrete and the epoxy coating. It penetrates into the cleaned substrate, filling tiny gaps and working to eliminate air bubbles that could otherwise lead to future delamination. In some cases, moisture mitigation barriers are added if there are concerns of hydrostatic pressure.

## Step 4: Epoxy Coating Installation

Once the primer has cured, we move on to applying the main epoxy layer. The material used varies depending on the system chosen—whether it’s a solid epoxy, decorative flake, or metallic finish. Our trained installers use rollers or squeegees to spread the epoxy evenly across the surface, ensuring consistent coverage and smooth texture. Decorative chips or pigments may be added at this stage before sealing.

## Step 5: Topcoat and Finishing Touches

After the epoxy layer, we apply a clear topcoat for added durability, UV protection (for sun-exposed areas), and chemical resistance. This protective layer enhances the appearance and extends the life of your floor. Depending on your preference, this can result in a high-gloss, satin, or matte finish.

## Step 6: Curing Time – Patience Pays Off

Once installation is complete, the floor needs adequate time to cure. Epoxy floors typically require [3 to 7 days of curing](https://www.thespruce.com/epoxy-flooring-basics-1824637) before they can handle heavy traffic. Light foot traffic may be allowed after 24-48 hours, but it’s essential to avoid vehicles or heavy machinery until full curing is achieved. This ensures that the coating reaches its maximum hardness and durability.

## Benefits of Choosing Epoxy Floors in Colorado Springs

Living in a dynamic climate like [Colorado Springs](/cities-we-serve/colorado-springs-concrete-floor-finishing/) means that flooring choices must stand up to wide ranges in temperature, snow, ice melt chemicals, and outdoor grit. Epoxy floors perform exceptionally well under these conditions. They’re also resistant to moisture, which makes them ideal for basements and garages exposed to fluctuating humidity levels.

Here are some advantages of epoxy flooring for Colorado Springs residents and business owners:

-   **Durability:** Resists cracking, yellowing, and staining, even under heavy use.
-   **Easy Maintenance:** Spills wipe away easily, and sealed surfaces resist dust and grime.
-   **Customization:** Wide range of colors, flakes, and designs to match your space aesthetic.
-   **Safety:** Optional slip-resistant textures enhance surface traction.
