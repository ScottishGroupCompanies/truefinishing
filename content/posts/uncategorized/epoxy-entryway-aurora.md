## The Rise of Epoxy Flooring

Epoxy flooring has seen significant growth due to its numerous benefits, appealing both to commercial and residential sectors. According to [Allied Market Research](https://www.alliedmarketresearch.com/epoxy-floor-coatings-market), the global epoxy flooring market is projected to reach approximately $9.8 billion by 2027, experiencing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of around 5.3% from 2020. This surge is attributed to consumer demand for long-lasting, visually appealing, and easy-to-maintain surfaces.

In the realm of residential applications, especially in high-traffic areas like entryways, epoxy flooring is unmatched. A study published in the [Journal of Coatings Technology and Research](https://www.springer.com/journal/11998) indicates that epoxy coatings can enhance the durability of such surfaces by up to 50%. This increased durability not only prolongs the lifespan of your flooring but also significantly reduces long-term maintenance costs.

## Benefits of Epoxy Entryways

At True Finishes, we understand that your entryway is more than a footpath; it is the gateway to your home. An epoxy entryway creates a strong first impression, reflecting your style while offering numerous practical benefits:

-   **Durability:** Epoxy coatings are highly resistant to wear and tear from foot traffic, making them ideal for entryways where durability is a priority.
-   **Aesthetics:** With a range of colors, patterns, and textures, epoxy flooring can be customized to fit the design of any home.
-   **Low Maintenance:** A simple sweep and mop are sufficient to keep epoxy floors looking new, saving you time and effort in upkeep.
-   **Safety:** The glossy finish of epoxy can be paired with anti-slip additives, ensuring safety without sacrificing style.

These advantages explain why, according to a [report by MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/PressReleases/epoxy-resin.asp), the demand for epoxy resin in construction, including in residential entryways, is expected to grow at an annual rate of about 7%.
