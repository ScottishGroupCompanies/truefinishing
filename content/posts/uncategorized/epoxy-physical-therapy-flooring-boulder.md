Epoxy flooring is a standout choice for physical therapy centers, combining strength, safety, and aesthetic appeal. Here’s why epoxy flooring is an invaluable addition to physical therapy centers in Boulder:

## Safety: Reducing Slip-and-Fall Incidents

Safety is a paramount concern in any healthcare setting, especially in physical therapy centers where patients are often rehabilitating from injuries. According to a [study published in the Journal of Safety Research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022437506000521), facilities equipped with epoxy flooring witnessed up to a 15% reduction in slip-and-fall incidents compared to those using traditional flooring systems. The slip-resistant surface of epoxy helps prevent accidents, fostering a safer environment for both patients and healthcare professionals.

## Durability: A Long-Lasting Solution

The bustling environment of a physical therapy center demands a flooring solution that can withstand constant foot traffic and equipment wear. Research from the [Epoxy Flooring Association](https://www.epoxyflooring.org/resources/benefits-epoxy-flooring/) indicates that epoxy flooring can extend a floor’s lifecycle by 10–15 years. This remarkable durability is complemented by a reduction in maintenance costs by approximately 30%, representing a cost-effective solution over time.

For more insights into our commercial flooring solutions, explore our [commercial floors](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) page.

## Hygiene: Maintaining a Clean Environment

Cleanliness remains a top priority in physical therapy centers, where hygiene directly impacts patient outcomes. A report published in the [American Journal of Infection Control](https://www.ajicjournal.org/article/S0196-6553(10)00572-8/fulltext) found that the seamless, non-porous nature of epoxy flooring improves hygiene ratings by up to 20% in healthcare facilities. The smooth surface eliminates cracks and crevices where germs and bacteria could otherwise accumulate, ensuring a more sterile environment for therapy activities.
