## What Is Metallic Gold Epoxy Flooring?

Metallic gold epoxy is a specialized decorative flooring system that incorporates mica or metallic pigments blended into an epoxy resin to create stunning, three-dimensional effects. The gold hues impart a sense of opulence and warmth often associated with upscale hotels, luxury boutiques, and premium urban residences.

Each installation is a one-of-a-kind masterpiece. No two metallic floors are ever exactly alike due to unique application techniques and curing patterns. From shimmering marbled effects to flowing river-like finishes, metallic gold epoxy transforms bland slabs of concrete into polished treasures underfoot.
