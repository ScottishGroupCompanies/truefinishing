## What Is Metallic Epoxy?

Metallic epoxy is a cutting-edge surface coating made by combining epoxy resin with specialized metallic pigments. When applied, it creates a glossy, fluid appearance that can resemble marble, flowing lava, or shimmering water. Each installation is a custom design, ensuring your Fort Collins bathroom is truly one of a kind.

This advanced floor system is not just about style. Thanks to its seamless and non-porous properties, metallic epoxy is also moisture-resistant, stain-resistant, and durable—making it ideal for bathrooms where humidity and water exposure are unavoidable.

Investing in a metallic epoxy bathroom can pay off, literally. ### 3\. Low Maintenance & Superior Hygiene

Traditional grout, tile, and hardwood solutions in bathrooms often require frequent cleaning and can harbor mold or bacteria.
