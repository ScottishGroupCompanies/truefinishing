### Enhanced Thermal Stability and Corrosion Resistance

According to a study from the [Journal of Coatings Technology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/app.201000123), incorporating graphite into epoxy coatings can enhance thermal stability and corrosion resistance by up to 50% compared to standard epoxy coatings. This enhancement means that industries can enjoy prolonged usability of their floors even in conditions where temperature fluctuations and chemical exposures are routine.

### Improved Electrical Conductivity

In environments where electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding is crucial, the role of our metallic graphite epoxy becomes even more significant. Research published on [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S003259101300251X) indicates that adding metallic additives like graphite improves electrical conductivity by about 30%. This makes our epoxy floor perfect for industries involved in electronics manufacturing and other technology-driven sectors.
