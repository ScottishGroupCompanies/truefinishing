## Why Choose Metallic Ruby Epoxy?

The allure of metallic finishes lies in their ability to transform ordinary surfaces into works of art. Metallic ruby epoxy, in particular, captivates with its rich, ruby-red hues and metallic sheen. These coatings are not just visually appealing; they are also engineered to withstand the demands of high-traffic areas. A study in the [Journal of Coatings Technology](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/15421406.2019.1573160) found that advanced metallic epoxy coatings can improve abrasion resistance by over 40% compared to standard epoxy systems. This makes them ideal for commercial environments like many in [Denver](/cities-we-serve/).

## The Growing Popularity of Decorative Epoxy Flooring

The appeal of decorative epoxy flooring is on the rise, driven by its aesthetic versatility and robustness. According to [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/decorative-epoxy-floor-coatings-market), this market segment is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate of approximately 8.5% through 2027. Urban centers, including Denver, are leading this trend as property owners seek out sophisticated design solutions that do not compromise on durability.

Surveys from [Floor Covering Weekly](https://www.floorcoveringweekly.com/flooring-news/flooring-market-trends/) further highlight the promise of metallic finishes. Nearly 65% of epoxy flooring installers in metropolitan areas, like Denver, express a strong preference for these finishes, citing enhanced aesthetic appeal and durability as key factors in their choice.
