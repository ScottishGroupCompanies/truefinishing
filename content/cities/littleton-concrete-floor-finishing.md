Downtown Littleton is an older main street along the South Platte. Ken Caryl is a different housing stock, more suburban and newer, up against the hogback. Treating them as one “south Denver” floor misses the prep difference: downtown is renovation, Ken Caryl is often a first or second finish on a newer slab.

Highlands Ranch sits next to Littleton and is not this page. The [recreation center case study](/case-study/highlands-ranch-recreation-center/) is the nearby moisture project, named as Highlands Ranch on purpose. Littleton garages still see south-metro de-icer, so [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) starts with grinding. Main-street retail that wants exposed concrete is [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/).
