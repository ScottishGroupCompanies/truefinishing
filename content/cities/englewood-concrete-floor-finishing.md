Englewood is an older city squeezed between Denver and Littleton. Broadway storefronts and houses toward the South Platte have been remodeled more than once. The concrete is often hiding under carpet, tile, or cutback adhesive, which is a prep job before it is a finish job.

Small commercial rooms on Broadway are a frequent [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) or [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) request once the old floor is gone. Houses that are getting new tile or vinyl usually need [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) because the original slab was never meant for large-format tile. The shop in Denver is a short drive north on Santa Fe and Lipan.
