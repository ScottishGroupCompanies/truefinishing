Westminster sits between Arvada, Broomfield, and Denver on US-36. The older city is ranch houses and small retail with slabs that have been covered before. Around Standley Lake and the newer north and east edges, the concrete is more recent. US-36 traffic brings the same de-icer into garages that Broomfield sees.

Older basements that are getting tile need [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/). Garages need [preparation](/floor-preparation/) and usually [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Retail and small offices that can leave the slab exposed are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). The Denver shop is south of here, so Westminster is a routine metro trip rather than an overnight one.
