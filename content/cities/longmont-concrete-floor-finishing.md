Longmont’s Main Street buildings are older commercial floors. The southwest side of town is where newer houses and small flex buildings have been added, between Boulder and Loveland. A brewery or shop downtown and a new garage in the southwest are different slabs that happen to share a city name.

Downtown renovations usually mean adhesive, old tile, or a coating that has to come off. That is [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/). New garages that take Boulder County winter brine are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Floors that should remain exposed are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). Anything that must be flat for tile is [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/).
