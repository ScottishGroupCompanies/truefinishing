Original Thornton and the newer north end are different eras of concrete. The older grids have basements and garages that have held coatings, paint, or carpet. The north end, toward Eastlake, is newer slabs in houses and small commercial buildings along the I-25 growth.

Both still see magnesium chloride from the interstate and the arterial roads. Garages are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) after the surface is ground. New commercial pads that should remain exposed are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). Basements going to tile or vinyl are [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) when the slab is out of flat. Thornton is a north-metro drive from the Lipan Street shop, often paired with Westminster.
