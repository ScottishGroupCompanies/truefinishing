Vail is a resort valley, not a Front Range suburb. Condo entries, parking structures, and house garages take snowmelt plus whatever salt and magnesium chloride the roads used that week. Interior floors in lodges and houses are often meant to stay exposed, sometimes over radiant heat.

Entries and garages are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) after grinding. Living areas are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) or a [microtopping](/floor-finishings/concrete-microtoppings/). Coverings over a valley slab should be tested first; that is [moisture mitigation](/floor-restoration/concrete-moisture-mitigation/). Aspen and Glenwood Springs are the other resort pages, and the conditions rhyme without being the same town.

Cracked or uneven concrete doesn’t just affect aesthetics—it can compromise safety and performance. Our concrete leveling and repair services restore smooth, even surfaces that eliminate trip hazards and strengthen your floors for lasting stability and reliability, even under heavy seasonal use.

### **Moisture Mitigation**

Vail’s high-altitude climate, snowmelt, and shifting moisture levels can damage unprotected concrete over time. Our advanced moisture mitigation systems protect your floors against vapor intrusion, delamination, and deterioration—keeping them strong, balanced, and ready for year-round mountain conditions.

Revitalize worn or damaged concrete with our professional floor restoration and resurfacing services. We remove stains, repair cracks, and refinish dull surfaces to restore their natural beauty and extend their lifespan—bringing back the refined look your space deserves.
