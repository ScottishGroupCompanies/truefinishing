Fort Collins is a full city on its own, not a Denver suburb with a different name. Old Town and midtown floors are often renovations. Southeast neighborhoods and new commercial buildings are newer slabs. Around campus, rental housing turns over and the floor underneath is whatever the last covering left behind.

Northern Colorado still gets freeze-thaw and clay. A rental turnover that needs tile usually needs [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) first. A restaurant or shop that wants a cleanable floor is [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). An office that wants exposed concrete is [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). The drive from the Denver shop is a real scheduling item, which is why this page exists separately from Denver.
