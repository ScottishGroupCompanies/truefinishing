Aspen sits high in the Roaring Fork Valley. Historic downtown floors and newer mountain houses do not see the same weather as a Denver warehouse. Snowmelt at entries, boot salt, and radiant-heated slabs show up on almost every walkthrough.

A heated slab can look dry and still need a moisture check before vinyl, wood, or a coating. That test is the [moisture mitigation](/floor-restoration/concrete-moisture-mitigation/) step. Decorative living spaces are often [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) or a [microtopping](/floor-finishings/concrete-microtoppings/). Mudrooms and garages that take salt are usually an [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) discussion after the surface is ground clean.

### **Polished Concrete**
Transform your Aspen property with sleek, reflective polished concrete that blends mountain elegance with modern durability. Perfect for luxury homes, retail boutiques, and hospitality spaces, our polished concrete floors are low-maintenance, built to withstand heavy use, and designed to complement Aspen’s refined alpine architecture.

**Epoxy Flooring**
Enhance your workspace with durable, chemical-resistant epoxy flooring—ideal for garages, workshops, and high-traffic commercial areas across Aspen. Our epoxy coatings deliver exceptional strength, slip resistance, and a smooth, professional finish that performs beautifully in both residential and industrial settings.

**Concrete Leveling & Repair**
Restore safety and stability to uneven or cracked surfaces. Our concrete leveling and repair services eliminate trip hazards, smooth out imperfections, and reinforce your floors for long-term reliability—essential in Aspen’s freeze-thaw climate.

**Moisture Mitigation**
Aspen’s variable weather and high-altitude moisture can take a toll on concrete. Our advanced moisture mitigation systems protect against vapor intrusion, delamination, and future damage—keeping your floors resilient and secure through every season.

**Decorative Overlays**
Bring character and texture to your space with our custom decorative overlays. Whether for mountain lodges, modern restaurants, or private residences, these designs infuse color, depth, and personality while maintaining the durability of expertly finished concrete.

**Floor Restoration**
Breathe new life into aging or damaged concrete floors. Our restoration and resurfacing services remove stains, repair cracks, and refinish your surface to reveal a renewed, polished look that enhances both aesthetics and longevity.

**Surface Preparation & Grinding**
Every flawless floor starts with meticulous preparation. We use precision grinding and surface prep to remove coatings, adhesives, and imperfections—creating a clean, even foundation ready for any new flooring system or finish.

**Commercial & Industrial Coatings**
Engineered for performance, our coatings protect Aspen’s commercial and industrial spaces from wear, chemicals, and impact. Ideal for warehouses, ski resort facilities, and heavy-use environments, these coatings combine safety, strength, and longevity.

**Concrete Sealing & Protection**
Preserve your floors against Aspen’s snow, ice, and moisture fluctuations with our premium sealants. Designed to resist weathering and staining, these finishes add beauty and protection—keeping your concrete surfaces strong and stunning year after year.
