Olde Town Arvada’s older retail slabs and the ranch-house garages toward Kipling Street and Wadsworth Boulevard are not the same kind of floor. Downtown spaces are often small, previously covered, and full of patchwork. The residential garages farther west take winter brine off Wadsworth and I-76.

Magnesium chloride tracked in on tires is a usual reason a garage coating fails if the slab is not cleaned and diamond-ground first. For those garages, start with [epoxy flooring](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Offices and shops that want the concrete itself as the finish are a [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) conversation. Floors that must be flat before tile or vinyl start with [concrete leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/).
