Castle Rock’s growth is mostly master-planned neighborhoods off I-25: The Meadows, Terrain, Founders, and the older downtown grid. A lot of the housing stock is new enough that the concrete has not been covered yet, which makes polish and coatings straightforward if the slab was finished well, and frustrating if it was not.

Douglas County clay swells and shrinks. Garage slabs and basement floors are where that shows up as ridges before large-format tile or vinyl. [Concrete leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) is the fix for flatness. Dust from grading and wind is a reason new slabs still get [ground](/floor-preparation/) before [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) or [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/).
