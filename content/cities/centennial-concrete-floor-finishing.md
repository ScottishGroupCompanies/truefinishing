Centennial stretches from older Southglenn retail to offices along the Denver Tech Center edge and houses around Dry Creek. Many of those buildings already have a floor covering. The concrete underneath is what decides whether the next finish is polish, a coating, or another covering.

A few minutes south, in Highlands Ranch rather than inside Centennial, the [Highlands Ranch Recreation Center](/case-study/highlands-ranch-recreation-center/) case study is a moisture-mitigation project. It is the closest published example of the vapor issues that show up under recreation and commercial slabs in this part of the south metro. Testing is [moisture mitigation](/floor-restoration/concrete-moisture-mitigation/). Exposed office floors are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/).
