Parker is Douglas County: The Pinery’s larger lots, Stonegate’s newer streets, and the commercial pads along the main roads. Most of the houses are new enough that the garage slab has never had a covering, and old enough that winter de-icer has already stained it.

Expansive clay under this part of the county moves. Basements and garages show it as high spots before tile or a coating. [Leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) fixes flatness for a covering. It does not stop the soil. [Epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) is the usual garage request after [grinding](/floor-preparation/). Living areas that stay as concrete are [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) or a [microtopping](/floor-finishings/concrete-microtoppings/).
