The shop is in Denver, at 2290 S Lipan Street. The city’s floors are not one slab. Baker, the Highlands, Congress Park, and Washington Park are full of older concrete that has already held carpet or tile. RiNo and Central Park add newer infill, sometimes still damp, sometimes already coated.

Published Denver-area work includes the [Sierra Trading Post](/case-study/sierra-trading-post/) leveling project and the [Epsilon](/case-study/epsilon/) carpet installation. Garage coatings across the city deal with magnesium chloride from Denver’s streets. Those start at [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/) and [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Open floors are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). Flatness for tile and vinyl is [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/).
