Broomfield sits between Boulder and Westminster on US-36. The FlatIron and Interlocken side is mostly newer commercial slabs and structured parking. The older neighborhoods are houses and small retail with floors that have already worn through carpet or vinyl once.

Parking structures and garages along the turnpike collect the same magnesium chloride as the rest of the north metro. Coatings there start with [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/) and [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Office floors that should stay exposed are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). Where a tenant finish needs a flat substrate for carpet tile or LVT, the step before the finish is [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/).
