Boulder floors run from older buildings near Pearl Street and Canyon Boulevard to offices and warehouses in East Boulder, with foothill freeze-thaw on top of ordinary Front Range clay. A downtown tenant improvement and a new slab off 28th Street do not get the same prep.

The published local project is [Moxy Boulder](/case-study/university-hotel-boulder-custom-concrete-prep-polish/): concrete preparation, leveling, overlays, a custom polish, plus tile and carpet in surrounding rooms. That mix is typical of a Boulder hospitality or retail floor. Garages and back-of-house rooms that need a coating are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Rooms that should stay exposed concrete are [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/).

### Polished Concrete

Create a sleek, reflective surface perfect for **Boulder’s modern offices, retail shops, and residential spaces**. Our **polished concrete floors** are easy to maintain, built for heavy use, and designed to complement the city’s signature mix of **mountain charm and contemporary design**.

### Epoxy Flooring

Protect your workspace with **durable, chemical- and abrasion-resistant epoxy flooring**—ideal for **industrial facilities, garages, breweries, and high-traffic commercial areas** across Boulder County. Engineered for long-term strength and visual appeal, our epoxy systems deliver a smooth, professional finish that lasts.

### Concrete Leveling & Repair

Uneven slabs and cracks can create safety hazards and impact your property’s value. Our **concrete leveling and repair services** restore stability and provide seamless, structurally sound finishes for **homes, offices, and commercial properties** throughout Boulder.

### Moisture Mitigation

Boulder’s wide temperature swings and changing humidity levels can lead to flooring issues over time. We install **advanced moisture barrier systems** that prevent vapor intrusion, protect coatings, and extend the life of your concrete floors.

### Decorative Overlays

Turn plain concrete into a **custom-designed statement piece**. Our **decorative overlays** add texture, color, and design flexibility—perfect for **restaurants, patios, galleries, and modern residences** that reflect Boulder’s creative aesthetic.

### Floor Restoration

Revive worn or damaged concrete with our **floor restoration and resurfacing services**. We repair cracks, smooth surfaces, and bring back your floor’s original strength and beauty, ensuring reliable performance for years to come.

### Surface Preparation & Grinding

A perfect floor starts with proper preparation. Our **surface grinding and preparation services** remove coatings, adhesives, and imperfections to create an even, clean base for any flooring or coating system.

### Commercial & Industrial Coatings

From **manufacturing plants to university facilities**, our **heavy-duty coating systems** are engineered to handle equipment, forklift traffic, and daily wear. Designed for longevity and safety, they keep your operation running efficiently.

### Concrete Sealing & Protection

Protect your investment from **Boulder’s freeze-thaw cycles and moisture exposure**. Our **high-performance sealants** guard against water damage, enhance your floor’s appearance, and extend the lifespan of your concrete surfaces.
