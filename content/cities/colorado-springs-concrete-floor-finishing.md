Colorado Springs is not a suburb of Denver. Downtown and the Old North End have older slabs. Powers, Briargate, and the Fountain side are newer construction at a range of elevations, with expansive soils that the city has dealt with for decades.

That spread means a floor walkthrough has to say which part of town the building is in. Older adhesive and tile thinset are a [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/) problem. Out-of-flat basements before tile are [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/). Garages and shops that want a coating are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/). Open commercial floors are often [polished concrete](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/).
