Loveland sits on the Big Thompson between Fort Collins and Longmont. Downtown floors are older. The west side is newer housing and commercial pads. Irrigated yards and a high water table in parts of the northern Front Range are why a slab can read wet even when the room looks dry.

That is a reason to test before [vinyl](/floor-finishings/vinyl-floor-finishing/), tile, or a coating, using the [moisture mitigation](/floor-restoration/concrete-moisture-mitigation/) process. Garages are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) after grinding. Open commercial concrete is [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). The trip is planned with Fort Collins more often than with Denver-day work.
