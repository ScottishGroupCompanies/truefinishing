Evergreen and Bergen Park are foothill houses, not metro warehouses. Driveways are steep, garages take snow and salt, and walkout basements see meltwater at the door. The floor system has to match that, not a plains retail spec.

Garage coatings are an [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) job after the salt film is ground off. Living areas that stay as concrete are [polished](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/) or a [microtopping](/floor-finishings/concrete-microtoppings/). Any covering over a walkout slab should start with a [moisture test](/floor-restoration/concrete-moisture-mitigation/), because a dry summer does not describe the slab in April.
