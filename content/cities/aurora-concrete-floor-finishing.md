Aurora is large enough that a floor in the original city and a floor near Southlands are different jobs. West of I-225, slabs are often older, previously covered, and full of adhesive. Farther east, the concrete is newer and the issue is more often flatness or construction dust than a failed 1970s topping.

Clay soils under much of the east metro move with moisture, so a floor that was flat at pour can show ridges later. When tile or vinyl is going down, [concrete leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) is the correction. Garages along the E-470 corridor still see magnesium chloride. Those get [floor preparation](/floor-preparation/) and, when a coating is the goal, [epoxy flooring](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/).
