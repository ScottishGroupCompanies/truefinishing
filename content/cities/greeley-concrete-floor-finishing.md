Greeley is a Weld County city with a real downtown, a university, and a west side that has been adding houses and small industrial buildings. Agricultural dust and expansive clay are the background conditions, not a Denver-neighborhood story with the city name swapped.

Shops and garages that need a cleanable surface are [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) after [grinding](/floor-preparation/). Open commercial floors can be [polished](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). Houses getting tile or vinyl on an older basement slab usually need [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/) so the new finish is not bridging dips. The crew comes from Denver, so Greeley is scheduled as a northern trip along with Fort Collins and Loveland.
