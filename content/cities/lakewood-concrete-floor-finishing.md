Lakewood runs from Belmar’s retail floors to mid-century ranches and the houses climbing toward Green Mountain. Belmar is a commercial finish problem: traffic, existing coverings, and a need for something that can be cleaned. The ranches are basement and garage slabs poured long before large-format tile existed.

Green Mountain gets more freeze-thaw than a floor east of Sheridan. Garages there are an [epoxy](/floor-finishings/epoxy-flooring/) job after grinding. Basements that need to be flat are [leveling](/floor-restoration/concrete-leveling/). Retail that can show the concrete is [polish](/floor-finishings/concrete-polishing/). The Denver shop on Lipan is the next neighborhood east, so Lakewood is one of the shorter trips.
