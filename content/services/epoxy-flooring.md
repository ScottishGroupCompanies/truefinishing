### True Guaranteed Quality

Quality is in all the details. A “True” quality jobs starts with proper inspection and analysis of the existing concrete to determine the best concrete coating system. Working with concrete can be tricky and we need to determine if there are any moisture issues in the concrete, whether or not a vapor barrier was installed, the condition and quality of the concrete, the hardness of the concrete, among other things. If the concrete checks out we can install one of our top epoxy floor coating systems based on how the floor will be used. If issues are present, then we can help you find the right solution to mitigate the problem such as a moisture vapor emission reduction system (MVERS).

For Epoxy, surface preparation is also critical to the performance of the floor and we always prep our floors with the right tools. All epoxy floors get diamond ground and/or shot blasted depending on application. Another cause of epoxy failure or de-lamination is the fine concrete dust that gets left of the surface after grinding. We make sure all dust is removed and the floor is super clean prior to application of the epoxy resin.

Knowing the things to watch out for and doing the job right are the keys to installing epoxy correctly. All of our jobs are backed by the **TRUE GUARANTEE**.

### Clean

**CLEAR EPOXY**

A clear coat of 100% solids zero-VOC epoxy is used to protect and enhance an existing concrete floor. Epoxy penetrates deeply into the concrete to provide years of protection and cleanability. Clear epoxy can also be used to protect decorative overlays or stained concrete floors that have been ground.

**SOLID-COLORED EPOXY**

Solid-Colored Epoxy really brings out the shine of a floor. A thicker layer of colored epoxy is poured on the floor an allowed to level out creating a smooth finish that is very easy to clean. This is the flooring choice for auto dealerships (as well as personal garages), air hangers, medical facilities, high-tech manufacturing, and other applications where a highly reflective and easy-to-clean surface is needed.

**SOLID-COLOR WITH AGGREGATE**

Adding integral quartz aggregate to solid-colored epoxy floors makes them extremely hard and long-wearing. The other benefit is the added texture which provide slip resistance. This is the best flooring type for auto repair shops, wash bays, manufacturing and grow facilities where water is used. It is highly durable while still being an economical choice.

### Decorative

**EPOXY FLAKE**

Epoxy flakes or vinyl chip floors are the top choice for residential garage application due to their durability and slip resistance in residential and commercial settings in Colorado. Epoxy flakes come is many different blends of colors and sizes. They not only come in blends of vinyl chips, but there are also specialty chips such as holographic, wood, and stone chips to expand your creative palette! Epoxy flake floors are very decorative and a good choice for high-end design and function.

**EPOXY QUARTZ**

Double-broadcast epoxy and urethane floor systems are very attractive and functional, making them popular for industrial, commercial, and institutional applications. Colored-quartz granules are double-broadcasted throughout the floor coating to create a variety of decorative looks that are extremely durable and require little maintenance.

**METALLIC EPOXY**

Metallic flooring systems use metallic powders that are dispersed and allowed to flow in a water-clear epoxy and polyaspartic coatings to provide a a real dazzling finish. This floor system is great for residential application as well as retail showrooms and lobbies. Decorative metallic flooring offers a one-of-a kind floor system that will really stand out!

### Functional

**URETHANE CEMENT**

Urethane Cement is a heavy-duty seamless flooring system which is installed at 1/8″ – 1/4″ in thickness depending on the application. The cement urethane coating system is ideal for them most demanding applications where thermal shock, impact, abrasion and chemical exposure is extreme. This flooring application is excellent for dairy and meat processing facilities, beverage production, locker rooms, and more.

**EPOXY MORTAR**

Epoxy mortar is applied by trowel and is intended to create a thick, very strong and impact resistant floor for industrial applications or anywhere where the concrete slab is highly eroded and in need of a new surface.

**MMA**

MMA (methyl methacrylate) is the ultimate flooring solution for rapid installations in low temperature and harsh environments. MMA flooring are extremely chemical, slip and water resistant making them the ideal choice for food and beverage applications, manufacturing facilities, gyms and sport centers.

## Direct answers

**Cost.** The [2026 epoxy cost guide](/epoxy-flooring/epoxy-flooring-cost-per-square-foot/) says professionally installed epoxy is about $4 to $12 per square foot on the Front Range. Garage flake systems, metallic floors, and heavy industrial mortars sit in different parts of that range. Those figures are the guide’s, not a quote for your slab.

The homepage states a 25-year warranty when a restoration project includes moisture mitigation. That warranty is not a universal epoxy lifespan.

**Pros.** A continuous surface, chemical resistance when the right system is chosen, and a floor that can be cleaned. The page lists solid, decorative, and functional systems, and jobs from about 1,000 to 100,000 square feet.

**Cons.** Epoxy is only as good as the prep. A wet slab or a contaminated surface will peel. Dark metallic floors show scratches. A garage that is not ground will not match a photo of one that was.

**Process.** Inspect the slab, including moisture and whether a vapor barrier exists. Diamond-grind or shot-blast. Remove the dust. Install the system the use of the floor calls for, including a moisture-vapor treatment when the test requires it.
