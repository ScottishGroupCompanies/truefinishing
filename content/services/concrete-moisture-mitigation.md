## Remediate High Moisture Levels in Concrete

True Finishing’s partnership with Ardex flooring solutions—combined with a shared commitment to consistent, high-quality results—ensures the highest standards in an industry often challenged by unpredictable variables.

In particular, this commitment applies to one of the most critical factors in achieving long-lasting, problem-free flooring: moisture control. To address this, the Ardex MC Moisture Control System offers a one- or two-coat epoxy resin solution, with or without a sand broadcast, specifically designed to suppress excessive moisture vapor.

These systems are especially useful when treating areas of new concrete where construction timelines are tight and allowing the concrete to fully dry may not be feasible. As a result, they provide an ideal solution for fast-paced commercial projects.

Furthermore, Ardex MC Moisture Control Systems are reactive epoxy systems that create an exceptionally hard surface, which bonds tenaciously to the substrate. Even better, these systems can reduce moisture emissions to safe, acceptable levels—even when applied to new concrete that’s only seven days old.

## Concrete Moisture Mitigation FAQ’s

Common questions asked about moisture mitigation for concrete.

What is moisture mitigation in concrete floors?

Moisture mitigation is the process of preventing moisture from passing through concrete slabs, which can damage flooring materials like tile, carpet, or hardwood. It ensures long-term durability and performance of your floors.

Why is moisture mitigation important before flooring installation?

Excess moisture can cause adhesives to fail and flooring to bubble, warp, or mold. Moisture mitigation protects your investment and ensures a longer-lasting floor.

What products do you use for moisture mitigation?

We use high-performance Ardex moisture mitigation systems, which come with a 25-year guarantee for peace of mind and long-term protection. We’ve seen it all. Use the best product for your moisture mitigation to guarantee long-lasting results. Give us a call! Have professional floor finishing contractors handle your next job.

How experienced are you with moisture mitigation systems?

We are the leaders in moisture mitigation in Denver, having successfully installed over 2,000,000 square feet of Ardex systems with zero failures.

How long does a moisture mitigation system take to install?

Most moisture mitigation systems can be installed in one to two days, depending on the size and condition of the concrete slab.

Can moisture mitigation be done on existing concrete floors?

Yes, moisture mitigation systems can be applied to existing concrete floors as long as proper surface preparation is done beforehand.
